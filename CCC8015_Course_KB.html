Notes on this catalogue

Every course listed is worth 3 credits. Class times and venues refer to Section 1 of the 2026-27 Term 2 offering and are subject to change; students must confirm them in the official enrolment system before registering.

Assessment weightings are not published in the public catalogue except for CCC8015. "Verify on course outline" means the weighting is not available from this source and must not be estimated.

Only these 28 courses are covered. Courses outside this list are outside the scope of this catalogue.
